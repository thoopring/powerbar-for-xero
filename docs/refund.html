Refund Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

30 days, no questions

If PowerBar Pro is not for you, email us within 30 days of your payment and we will refund it in full. You do not have to explain why, and you do not have to have stopped using it.

This applies to your first payment on a subscription, monthly or annual.

After 30 days

Cancel any time from the link in your purchase receipt. Your Pro features keep working until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not billed again. We do not automatically refund part-used periods, with two exceptions:

  • A renewal you did not intend. If an annual or monthly renewal caught you out and you have not used Pro since it renewed, ask and we will refund it.
  • A Pro feature we withdraw. If we discontinue a Pro feature during a period you have paid for, we refund the unused part.

Before you buy

The free features need no payment and no account, so you can see how the extension behaves in your own Xero before spending anything. If you are unsure whether Pro solves your problem, ask us first — we would rather talk you out of a purchase than process a refund.

How to request one

Open an issue at github.com/thoopring/powerbar-for-xero/issues, or reply to your Lemon Squeezy purchase receipt. Include the email address used at purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record, and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Statutory rights

This policy is in addition to any refund or cancellation rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you. Nothing here limits them.
